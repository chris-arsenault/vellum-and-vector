# Fantasy cities, cards and Civilization wonders

These references answer three different design questions. What makes a settlement possible in the first place? Who gets to change it? What can one extraordinary work do that a district of ordinary buildings cannot? The observations below come from the cited works. The proposed atlas adaptations are original design choices.

## Cities with a rule a visitor can discover

| City | What the source supplies | What to borrow for a map |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armada, *The Scar* | Captured vessels form the ground of a floating city. New arrivals can be brought there against their will. | A new piece of habitable land can arrive **already occupied**. Joining two hulls creates neighbors with histories and a contested crossing. A harbor full of boats would miss the premise. [Publisher's description](https://penguinrandomhouselibrary.com/book/?isbn=9780553551310) |
| Sharn, *Eberron* | Skycoaches serve different levels. Their operation depends on the local manifest zone. Operators and fares distinguish journeys through the same city. | Found on two habitable ledges. Give the connector a landing at each end, an operator and a service area. The expensive upper landing and the foot of the public stair can become entirely different destinations. [Keith Baker on Sharn's skycoaches](https://keith-baker.com/ifaq-skycoaches/) |
| Nargothrond, Tolkien's legendarium | Tolkien drew the fortified entrance to an underground stronghold. The later bridge across the river helped bring about its fall. | A public work can reverse the founding advantage. An exposed bridge may enrich the outer market while ending the inner town's isolation. Keep both mouths on the map after that choice. [Tolkien Estate, drawing and commentary](https://www.tolkienestate.com/painting/the-silmarillion/) |
| Sigil, *Planescape* | The City of Doors trades through portals. The Lower Ward's routes to the Lower Planes help explain its industrial character. | The *other end* of a connection matters. A distant destination can support a warehouse or embassy on an otherwise unremarkable inland street. In this atlas a crossing needs the local Veil conditions and a keeper. [Dungeons & Dragons, Sigil and its wards](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1576-what-to-know-before-you-visit-sigil-the-city-at) |
| Ravnica | Guilds organize law, experimentation, military force, worship and labor across a city built on earlier layers. Their powers overlap geographically. | Give institutions jurisdiction over particular decisions. A parcel may belong to a household while another body controls its water access. Where jurisdictions meet, give people a physical place to petition, exchange or bypass them. [Wizards, Ravnica](https://magic.wizards.com/en/story/ravnica-plane) |
| Naktamun, *Amonkhet* | During reconstruction, the living need the labor of the dead. Formerly subordinated workers bargain for freedom. Repair spreads from the center while farmers reopen canals. | Build social power into who can withhold labor and who may occupy land. If those rights change, later construction should be able to make a different district beside the inherited one. This also suggests a town where separate communities jointly maintain a piece of infrastructure neither can replace. [Wizards, rebuilding Naktamun](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-aetherdrift-part-2) |

Armada and Naktamun make a useful pair. A new hull and a newly independent labor association both enlarge the town's possibilities. Only the first needs more ground. A founding catalog should be able to describe both kinds of change.

**Menzoberranzan** offers two separable ideas. A vast cavern and its luminous central spire give people physical bearings. A ruling faith determines which worship people can practice openly. Borrowing only the glowing skyline would miss where someone can safely congregate. For the atlas, the same cavern could instead shelter a republic of exiles, while a coercive religious order could operate on open farmland. [Dungeons & Dragons, Menzoberranzan](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1028-welcome-to-menzoberranzan-the-drow-city-of)

**Otoh Gunga and the Merrow Lanes** pose different underwater questions. Otoh Gunga's grown structures enclose breathable atmospheres. Lorwyn's merrow travel through submerged routes and operate shops along the bank for land dwellers. The first explains how an interior can be inhabited; the second explains where two populations meet. A generator needs both answers before a submerged city can function. Borrow the distinction and ground the means of habitation in the generated world. [Star Wars Databank](https://www.starwars.com/databank/otoh-gunga) · [Wizards, the Merrow Lanes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/planeswalkers-guide-to-lorwyn-eclipsed)

**A historical control for deliberate geometry.** The Round City of Baghdad was laid out on the ground before building. Four gates faced routes named for different destinations, with residential and commercial ground between the fortifications and the seat of government. This suggests a useful way to model a compass charter: protect bearings and rights of passage from the first occupation, then let builders fill the arcs over time. A creator can recognize the organizing decision even on a half-built map. [Archnet, Madina al-Mansur al-Mudawwara](https://www.archnet.org/sites/3826)

## What individual Magic cards contribute

*Conqueror's Galleon* turns over to *Conqueror's Foothold*. Ship and establishment have one identity across a change of use. *Search for Azcanta* turns an inquiry into a specific submerged place. For the atlas these suggest an inhabited vessel that later becomes a street, and a survey station that discovers the site of a great work after founding. Discovery can change who travels to a place long before anyone can live there. [Ixalan card fronts and backs](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/card-image-gallery/ixalan)

*Growing Rites of Itlimoc* becomes land through a gathered community of creatures. The organizing idea for a grove town is a community keeping something alive together. Its earliest recognizable feature may be a protected clearing between homes. The trees may only reach architectural scale generations later. *Thaumatic Compass* becomes the *Spires of Orazca*, suggesting another distinction: a device used repeatedly to find one's way can give rise to a permanent point of orientation. [Ixalan release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/ixalan-release-notes)

*Monumental Henge* gives its controller access to historic cards. A monument can have a public use on a particular day: witnesses come to settle a claim, hear testimony or retrieve an old charter. Its approach, assembly ground and people charged with keeping records would matter even if its stones never move. [Modern Horizons 3 release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/modern-horizons-3-release-notes)

## What Civilization wonders contribute

| Precedent | Design lesson | Original atlas application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Venetian Arsenal | In Civ VI the coastal work depends on an industrial neighbor. The Civilopedia connects its historical yards to both military and merchant fleets. Its physical working edge is as important as its gate. | A fleet yard consists of tidal slips, a timber entrance and a fitting basin. Closing its water approach strands other people's property. [Venetian Arsenal, in-game entry](https://www.civilopedia.net/en-US/rise-and-fall/wonders/building_venetian_arsenal/) |
| Huey Teocalli | The game's lake-and-land adjacency makes the shore part of the monument. The Civilopedia identifies the paired dedicatory purposes of the historical temple. | An island sanctuary has a landing, a mainland approach and an assembly on water. A city built against its processional shore can lose the very space that made the ceremony possible. [Huey Teocalli, in-game entry](https://www.civilopedia.net/en-US/rise-and-fall/wonders/building_huey_teocalli/) |
| Great Bath | Its floodplain site lets the work change what a flood does along a river. Its area of influence extends well beyond the structure. | A staircase of flood courts admits water into common basins; its outlet and the homes downstream matter as much as the headworks. [Gathering Storm manual, World Wonders](https://cdn.2kgames.com/manuals/civilization-vi/gathering_storm/2KGMKT_CIV6_Gathering_Storm_PC_Online_Manual_V7_US.pdf) |
| Meenakshi Temple | The game requires an established religious institution as well as a suitable neighboring site. | A dispersed pilgrimage community could commission a single destination. A newly wealthy capital with no such constituency might choose an entirely different work. [Gathering Storm manual, World Wonders](https://cdn.2kgames.com/manuals/civilization-vi/gathering_storm/2KGMKT_CIV6_Gathering_Storm_PC_Online_Manual_V7_US.pdf) |

Civilization gives a work a site, a recognizable footprint and consequences outside it. Its one-per-world race serves a competitive strategy game. An atlas can instead make two monumental archives worth visiting because their collections, custodians and sites differ. For each proposed work, ask where a visitor enters, what happens there, and what part of the town would change if it closed.
