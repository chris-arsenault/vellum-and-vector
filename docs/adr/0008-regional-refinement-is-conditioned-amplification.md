# 0008 — Independent regional development through shared process owners

Adopted for independent maps under [ADR 0005](0005-scale-contract.md).
The user's 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-10 corrections supersede earlier parent-blur
fitting, fixed crossings, boundary heights and captured-field floors.

## Decision

A child develops its captured present into an independent map. It shares coordinates
and completed history with its source, but its elevation, coast, courses and place
geometry may change. Named identity and existence survive; numerical agreement and
boundary reconciliation are not requirements. A child does not change its parent
or siblings, and later parent changes do not replace its pinned inputs.

The child uses the same physical field, triangular lattice, Ground, material cargo,
fluid stores, magic inventories and MapScale as world generation. Immutable input
resources may be shared; working numerical state and published records belong to
the child. There is no regional material representation, unit vocabulary or recovery
format.

Field allocation and lattice geometry use the compact continuous chart of the selected
footprint. Catalog footprints and published features stay in the world's canonical
chart. Shared coordinate utilities place captured inputs and spatial predicates in the
working chart, and publication splits geometry back at the world seam. Coordinate
aliases use the world's width; they do not join a short region's outer lattice edges.
Only a frame spanning the cylinder has periodic adjacency.

## Feature-owned execution

World and regional execution resolve selected features through the same composition
path and process runner. Features own their executable operations and regional
disposition: use a shared implementation, select a specialization, or retain captured
formation instead of replaying it. Disabled features contribute no operations.
The regional coordinator supplies ordering and local work exposure.

A specialization keeps the shared consumer contract. Regional drainage can supply
captured incoming rivers to the shared water owners; incision, groundwater and
sediment read shared river records and routing quantities. Regional climate starts with
captured atmospheric inputs and resolves their response to current terrain.

New influences join shared question responders. New materials use existing Ground
responses and transfers. A new carrier still needs an explicit representation and
causal execution position; composition does not infer those relationships.

## Capture, development and refresh

Initialization captures current Ground packages and their cargo, water and living
stores, structure, source material and named facts. Present ecological cohorts and
seed banks are inherited rather than reconstructed from aggregate tree cover.
Existing magic source identities remain context, while their current eligibility
depends on retained material, carriers and maintenance.
Capture follows the pinned resources published by each owner. Working bands, published
resources and captured inputs share identifiers, units and vertical datum. Shared sampling
creates child-owned storage; it does not rename, re-encode or rebase fields. Extensive
quantities adjust for represented area during sampling, and material packages use one donor.
There is no separate regional channel allowlist. Retained ice evidence survives thaw, and the atmosphere carries its actual
applied heat and fog operands for replacement at the child's present.

Initial local relief starts at the captured surface. Complete retained beds supply their
inclined contacts before drainage organization asks which faces yield. Valley placement
resolves the captured cut share through connected, material-responsive ground; contacts
then resolve from the same captured beds on the occupied facets. Grain, bed dip and exposed
support act through the ordinary Ground responses. Captured faults and sheet contacts
provide local fracture organization without replaying tectonic motion.
Ground displacement moves retained contacts, and excavation exposes and
transfers the material it reaches. This initialization runs once. Subsequent rounds
retain and work its result through shared erosion, mass wasting, sediment, coast,
ice and groundwater owners; they do not rebuild it from a parent-height fit.

Imported crust starts from its captured mechanical support. The shared flexure owner
evaluates the imported state at the new map scale once, then responds to changes from
that settled reference. A smaller frame does not itself lift the seabed or lower a
plateau. New loads and changes in crust still deform ground through its owner.

Local ecological response uses the shared soil, vegetation, toxicity, animal and
organic-material kernels. Development exposure is a local relaxation control, not
elapsed world time. It does not increase canopy age or thermally mature fossil
carbon. Actual new cover can bury organic material, and later removal can expose it.

Magical refresh derives fields and meetings from current inventories and media.
It cannot replenish stocks, restore old access, deposit ground or append history.
Explicit local material response uses shared Ground operations. Retained organic
charge follows actual burial and pickup. Historical formation, world epochs and
human eras are not replayed.

Derived climate, routing, classifications and publication are separate from material
development. Captured atmospheric contributions are replaced rather than added twice.
Selected current queries share the map's operation revision. A query resolves its own
derived dependencies; ecology therefore reads the current channel and flood environment.
Regional inflow is a Strategy for the shared current-water query. Groundwater development
uses explicit exposure; current water reads never recharge its retained store.
External maritime context enters through the working frame; interior coast distance
follows the child's current shoreline. The finite frame is a computational boundary,
not a seam that must match another map.

The captured atmosphere retains the world's dimensional frame for altitude response
and continentality. Those lengths resolve through `MapScale` against the world extent;
shrinking a child map does not by itself intensify cooling per metre. Terrain and wind
detail still resolve at the child's scale. New material uses the captured run's completed
epoch count as its present date, without adding an epoch or consulting a later parent run.

## Named geography and publication

Established features contribute through the existing cause registry. Captured rivers
supply context and inflow; settlements and landmarks retain their identities while
their local geometry can develop. Existing place realization remains part of
refinement. It does not introduce a new human era; how the inherited people live
on the child is decided in
[ADR 0012 (people live on the land's layers)](0012-people-live-on-the-lands-layers.md).
Accepted new events dispatch one-time catalogue Commands. Captured disaster records
do not restore sediment, reopen an ice breach or repeat damage. Maintained works instead
declare current influences, including water withdrawals and sink losses, through shared
quantity responders. Captured relief is context for local counterpart recognition;
place realization does not enforce parent elevation bounds.

The shared projection, extraction and naming rules publish the child's geography
at its own scale. Descendants capture that result through the same ownership and
resource contracts. Navigation between maps remains explicit; zoom stays on the
open map.

## Verification

Pure contracts cover input meaning, independent ownership, material operations and
actual feature dispatch. They do not compare parent and child samples, budgets or
blurred surfaces. Representative saved-parent refinements are inspected for useful
terrain, current water and coasts, retained material and transformed inherited
structure. Passing contracts alone does not establish geographic quality.

Implementation lives in the feature definitions, shared owners and
`packages/generation/src/regional`. Parameters remain in code; this record describes
responsibilities and boundaries rather than a second schedule.
